# LightsOut.TheWardenTrial
Official Volunteer Portal for LIGHTS OUT: The Warden Trial (Level 01). A real-life extreme horror sport designed to test raw human courage in complete darkness. Step into the woods, follow the Gamemaster's rules, and survive the Hush. ⚠️ WARNING: Intense psychological terror—strictly not for the faint of heart.


THE INVITATION
Think you have the nerve when the lights go out? You are officially summoned to register for LIGHTS OUT: The Warden Trial — Level 01. This is not a screen. This is not a movie. This is a real-life survival horror sport built to push your instincts, focus, and courage to the absolute limit.

ABOUT THE TRIAL
Hosted live by the Gamemaster, Level 01 drops volunteers into an unforgiving night environment with minimal gear and strict isolation rules. Navigate the dark, recover the embers, and evade The Hush—because out here, hesitation costs you the trial. Only those who keep their composure make it out.

⚠️ EXTREME WARNING: REAL ENVIRONMENTAL & PSYCHOLOGICAL DANGER
This trial takes place in an untamed, open wilderness—you will face genuine physical hazards from the wild forest, uneven terrain, natural obstacles, and harsh night elements, alongside pitch-black environments, disorienting audio, relentless psychological pressure, and sudden physical encounters. The forest is real, and the danger is real. If you are faint-hearted, soft-hearted, prone to panic attacks, physically unprepared for rough terrain, or easily overwhelmed by intense fear, DO NOT REGISTER. Proceed only if you are ready to prove what you are made of.
