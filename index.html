The Twist · Isolation & Infection

Strict Solo Rule

Runners stay 15+ metres apart. Teaming up triggers an automatic proximity penalty.

The Proxy Infection

The first 2 runners snuffed don't leave. They become Proxies, The Hush's minions, and hunt the rest.

The Dim Rule

Shine your narrow beam on The Hush and you have 3 seconds to look away. Stare longer and a Mental Freeze forces you to drop an Ember.

The Stakes · Loadouts & The Penalty

Runner Loadout

The Hush Loadout

  • Dark hooded robe, cracked lantern mask

⚠ Mandatory Medical & Safety Notice

  • 18+ only. Everyone consents and plays voluntarily.
  • No heart conditions, pacemakers, epilepsy, severe asthma, recent injuries or pregnancy.
  • No alcohol or intoxicants. Pads on forearm or calf only, never the chest, neck, spine or head.
  • Daylight terrain walkthrough before every game. Crew spotters watch the perimeter.
  • Shout "STOP GAME" to freeze everything. Nobody is ever penalised for using it.

Apply as a Volunteer Runner

The Hush is played by a trusted crew member, not a competing runner, so the hunt stays fair and the controller stays in safe hands.
